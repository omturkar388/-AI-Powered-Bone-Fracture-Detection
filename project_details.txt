What each folder is for:
- data/raw/: original dataset; don't edit the source images.
- data/processed/: cleaned or organized data.
- src/: training, prediction, and evaluation code.
- models/: trained model files.
- reports/: metrics, plots, and evaluation results.
- templates/ and static/: website interface and styling.
We will keep large datasets, trained weights, and private uploaded X-rays out of GitHub.
